I am an undergraduate researcher pursuing an honors degree in Computer Science (IT1) at the School of Information and Communication Technology (SoICT), Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST).

My academic pursuits converge at the intersection of Distributed Systems & Big Data Graph Processing and Program Analysis for Software Security. At the SEDS Lab, mentored by Dr. Tran Van Dang, I investigate fine-grained provenance recording and performance acceleration within distributed graph computation frameworks (Apache Spark, GraphFrames). Concurrently, at the AI4Code Research Laboratory led by Assoc. Prof. Huynh Quyet Thang, I explore deep semantic vulnerability prediction in systems codebases through control-flow and graph representations.

Grounding my research in rigorous fundamentals, I actively practice competitive programming and high-performance systems engineering in modern C++17, emphasizing cache-friendly data layouts and low-latency algorithmic designs.
