Efficient Distributed Graph Processing and Provenance Tracking in Large-Scale Analytics

Minh Quan Dang, Tran Van Dang (Advisor)

Software Engineering and Decentralized Systems (SEDS) Lab, School of Information and Communication Technology, HUST.

Abstract & Research Focus: Fine-grained data provenance in large-scale iterative graph computation is essential for debugging, compliance, and lineage attribution, yet traditional provenance recording introduces prohibitive execution and storage overheads. In this work, we investigate a resilient provenance tracking architecture built on Apache Spark and GraphFrames. By decoupling intermediate lineage emission from the core iterative execution loop and applying run-length and bit-packed vector compression, our framework aims to preserve fine-grained transformation traces across iterative algorithms (PageRank, Connected Components, Label Propagation) with minimal runtime penalty.

Research in Progress · AI4Code Lab AI4Code Lab · 2026

Deep Semantic Graph Representation for Vulnerability Prediction in C/C++ Systems Codebases

Minh Quan Dang, Huynh Quyet Thang (Advisor)

AI4Code Research Laboratory, Hanoi University of Science and Technology.

Abstract & Research Focus: Vulnerabilities in low-level systems software predominantly stem from intricate control- and data-flow interactions that escape surface-level token models. We investigate a hybrid representation linking Abstract Syntax Tree (AST) tokens, control-flow edges, and program dependence graphs to model vulnerability patterns in C/C++ projects, evaluating empirical defect classification against CVE benchmarks.
